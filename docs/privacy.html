Fuel Saver — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 June 2026

Fuel Saver ("the app") is designed to respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Fuel Saver does not require an account, does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking, and does not sell or share your personal information. Your vehicles and settings stay on your device.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location while you are using the app to find fuel stations and EV chargers near you. Your approximate location is sent to the data providers listed below solely to return nearby results. Your location is not stored on our servers, is not linked to your identity, and is never used to track you across apps or websites. You can change or revoke location access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Information stored on your device

Your garage (vehicles and fuel types), preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This information is not transmitted to us.

Data providers

To show prices and related information, the app queries third‑party services with your approximate location:

  • Queensland Government fuel price reporting scheme (fuel prices)
  • Open Charge Map (EV charging locations)
  • Brisbane City Council open data (planned road closures)

These providers have their own privacy practices, which apply to the requests the app makes on your behalf.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
